          • Columns. BITS ID, Course and Total Marks, in any order. Headings such as “Student’s BITS
          ID” or “Total Marks (out of 100)” are recognised. Other columns are ignored.
            • 
        
          • Marks. Whole numbers from 0 to 100. Round fractions to the nearest whole number before
          uploading (80.2 → 80, 80.5 → 81). If decimals remain, the console asks how to grade them.
            • 
        
          • NC students. Students who did not appear for the examination and are to receive NC should
          not be in the file. Rows with blank or text marks such as “AB” are listed and left out.
            • 
        
          • Several courses. One file can hold many courses; you grade and export them one at a time.
        
            • 
        
          • Privacy. The file is read inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded.
